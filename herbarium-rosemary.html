Rosemary

Salvia rosmarinus (formerly Rosmarinus officinalis)

🌿 Herb🍴 Culinary✣ Ornamental☀ Drought-tolerant
🌿
Herbarium Dossier · Sykerbos
1
🌿

Meet the plant

A woody perennial herb prized for its aroma, flavour, and resilience. Rosemary is both a culinary herb and an ornamental shrub.

2
🌍

Origins & habitat

Native to the Mediterranean region, rosemary naturally grows on sunny, rocky, free-draining slopes shaped by long, dry summers.

3
⚱

History & uses

Used for centuries in cooking, fragrance, gardens, and traditional herbal practice. Its aromatic foliage and flowers also support pollinators.

4
⚙

Unique abilities

Narrow, leathery leaves, rolled-under margins and fine hairs help reduce water loss. Glandular trichomes produce many of rosemary’s aromatic compounds.

5
💧

Water strategy

Rosemary prefers bright light and well-drained roots. It usually tolerates drying better than many soft herbs, but dislikes staying wet for too long.

6
▥

What Sykerbos may see

Expect a steadier dry-down curve than basil and a higher risk from repeated overwatering than from brief dryness. Over time, Sykerbos can compare irrigation events with the plant’s individual water-use pattern.

7
💡

Fun fact

The name rosemary is traditionally linked to the phrase “dew of the sea,” reflecting its Mediterranean coastal associations. It has also long been connected with memory and remembrance.
