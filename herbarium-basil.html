Basil

Ocimum basilicum

🌿 Herb🍴 Culinary☀ Warm-season💧 Moderate water
🌿
Herbarium Dossier · Sykerbos
1
🌿

Meet the plant

A tender, aromatic annual commonly grown for fresh leaves. Basil grows quickly in warmth and can respond visibly when water becomes limiting.

2
🌍

Origins & habitat

Basil is associated with tropical and subtropical regions of Asia and Africa and thrives in warm conditions with fertile, well-drained soil.

3
⚱

History & uses

Cultivated for centuries in food, fragrance, and traditional practices, basil is central to many cuisines and is especially valued for its fresh aromatic leaves.

4
⚙

Unique abilities

Soft leaves and rapid growth support high productivity, but also mean basil can lose water quickly. Its strong aroma comes from a complex mixture of volatile oils.

5
💧

Water strategy

Basil prefers fairly even moisture and usually performs poorly when repeatedly pushed very dry. Good drainage still matters because saturated roots can also cause stress.

6
▥

What Sykerbos may see

Basil may show a relatively fast dry-down curve, strong changes on hot days, and clear irrigation responses. Repeated cycles could help Sykerbos learn when the plant approaches wilt.

7
💡

Fun fact

Different basil cultivars can smell of clove, citrus, cinnamon, anise, or even licorice because their aromatic oil profiles vary widely.
