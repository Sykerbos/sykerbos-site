How does Sykerbos read water reserve?

Your pot gets heavier when you water it and lighter as water leaves. Sykerbos compares its current weight with its saved full and dry weights.

Reserve days use this pot’s measured higher-use days. Weight loss includes evaporation. Open a plant’s measurements to see the figures behind its estimate.
