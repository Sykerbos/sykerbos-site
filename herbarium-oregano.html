Oregano

Origanum vulgare

🌿 Herb🍴 Culinary☀ Mediterranean◌ Dry-down tolerant
🌿
Herbarium Dossier · Sykerbos
1
🌿

Meet the plant

A hardy aromatic perennial with small leaves and a spreading habit. Oregano is valued for its intense flavour and tolerance of lean, sunny conditions.

2
🌍

Origins & habitat

Oregano is native across much of Europe and western Asia and is strongly associated with dry, sunny Mediterranean landscapes.

3
⚱

History & uses

Used extensively in Mediterranean cooking, oregano has also been grown for fragrance and traditional herbal uses for generations.

4
⚙

Unique abilities

Small aromatic leaves, perennial roots, and tolerance of nutrient-poor soils help oregano persist through hot, dry periods.

5
💧

Water strategy

Oregano prefers free drainage and usually benefits from drying between irrigations. Constantly wet soil can reduce root health and flavour quality.

6
▥

What Sykerbos may see

Its water-use curve may resemble thyme or rosemary more than basil, with slower dry-down and larger acceptable gaps between irrigations.

7
💡

Fun fact

The flavour called “oregano” can come from several related species, and plants from different regions may have noticeably different aromatic profiles.
