Thyme

Thymus vulgaris

🌿 Herb🍴 Culinary☀ Mediterranean◌ Drought-tolerant
🌱
Herbarium Dossier · Sykerbos
1
🌿

Meet the plant

A compact woody perennial with tiny aromatic leaves. Thyme is well adapted to bright, dry conditions and low-water Mediterranean environments.

2
🌍

Origins & habitat

Common thyme is native to the western Mediterranean region, where it grows in sunny, stony ground and other free-draining habitats.

3
⚱

History & uses

Used in cooking, fragrance, and traditional herbal practice for centuries, thyme remains a staple herb in Mediterranean and European cuisines.

4
⚙

Unique abilities

Its very small leaves reduce exposed surface area, while aromatic oils and woody growth help it tolerate heat and periodic dryness.

5
💧

Water strategy

Thyme prefers to dry between irrigations and is vulnerable to persistently wet, poorly aerated roots. Containers should drain freely.

6
▥

What Sykerbos may see

Expect a slow, relatively shallow water-use pattern compared with lush herbs such as basil or mint. Long periods of little weight change may be completely normal.

7
💡

Fun fact

The genus name Thymus has ancient roots and has long been associated with aromatic plants used in food, ritual, and medicine.
