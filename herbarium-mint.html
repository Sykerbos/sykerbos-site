Mint

Mentha × piperita

🌿 Herb🍴 Culinary❄ Perennial💧 Moisture-loving
🍃
Herbarium Dossier · Sykerbos
1
🌿

Meet the plant

A vigorous aromatic perennial that spreads readily and produces soft, fast-growing shoots. Peppermint is a hybrid mint grown worldwide.

2
🌍

Origins & habitat

Mints favour moist soils and often occur naturally near streams, ditches, and damp ground across temperate regions.

3
⚱

History & uses

Mint has a long history in food, tea, fragrance, and traditional remedies. Peppermint became especially popular for its cooling menthol-rich aroma.

4
⚙

Unique abilities

Underground stems allow mint to spread aggressively, while soft leaves support rapid growth. Glandular tissues produce menthol and other aromatic compounds.

5
💧

Water strategy

Mint generally prefers more consistent moisture than Mediterranean herbs. It tolerates brief drying, but prolonged dryness can quickly reduce leaf quality and growth.

6
▥

What Sykerbos may see

Mint may show higher and more continuous water use, especially in warm bright conditions. A falling dry-down rate could also reveal slowing growth or cooler conditions.

7
💡

Fun fact

Peppermint is a natural hybrid between watermint and spearmint, combining traits from both parent species.
