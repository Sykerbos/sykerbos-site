Before the first watering cycle

Tare the empty VyTile first.

Put VyTile on a firm, level surface. If you’ll use a saucer, place the empty, dry saucer on the tile now. Keep the pot off the tile, then use the tare control to set this starting load to zero.

Keep the same saucer in place during measurement. The imagery shows the design concept.

First cycle · 1 of 4

Water slowly and evenly.

With the pot off VyTile, water the mix gently until drainage begins. If the mix is very dry, pause and water again so it wets throughout.

The illustrations use basil to show the sequence. Follow your plant’s care needs and use a pot with drainage holes.

First cycle · 2 of 4

Let excess water drain.

Let the pot drain freely over the sink until the initial runoff finishes. Empty any collected runoff and dry the outside of the pot before placing it on VyTile.

Start with a well-watered, freely drained pot. Keep runoff away from the tile.

First cycle · 3 of 4

Place the drained pot on VyTile.

Centre the whole pot on the tared tile, or in the saucer you included when taring. Let the reading settle without touching the pot. This is the starting point for the first wet reference.

Wet reference

Wait for a steady reading.

The settled weight of the well-watered, freely drained pot gives Sykerbos a reference for the first dry-down.

Example · stable 1,284 g 22.4 °C · illustrative reading

This setup preview shows a sample reading. With your device, wait for the actual weight to settle before using it as a reference.

First cycle · 4 of 4

Follow the first dry-down.

Leave Kitchen Rosemary on VyTile in its growing spot. Follow the weight as the pot dries. Repeated watering and dry-down cycles will build a picture of this plant’s behaviour.
